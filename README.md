**Project Overview**

LUXORA is a premium fashion e-commerce experience designed to provide customers with an elegant, intuitive, and seamless online shopping journey.

The project focuses on luxury fashion merchandising, product presentation, interactive shopping components, and a responsive user interface.

The design features a sophisticated visual identity with warm ivory backgrounds, champagne gold accents, elegant typography, and premium product photography.

**Design Objectives**

- Create an elegant and modern fashion shopping experience.
- Improve product discovery and browsing.
- Present products through detailed descriptions and image galleries.
- Design interactive color and size selection components.
- Provide convenient wishlist and shopping cart experiences.
- Create a smooth checkout and order confirmation journey.
- Maintain responsive layouts across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

**Screens Designed**

The project consists of eight major screens:

No.| Screen| Description
01| Home / Storefront| Fashion storefront and featured collections
02| Product Catalog| Product browsing, filtering, and sorting
03| Product Detail| Detailed product information and shopping actions
04| Wishlist| Saved products and wishlist management
05| Shopping Bag| Cart management and order summary
06| Checkout| Shipping, delivery, and payment interface
07| Order Confirmation| Successful order placement experience
08| Product Reviews| Customer ratings and reviews

**Key UI/UX Features**

- Premium fashion-oriented visual identity.
- Detailed product descriptions and pricing.
- Product image galleries and photography.
- Customer ratings and reviews.
- Interactive color swatches.
- Size selector chips.
- Wishlist bookmarking.
- Quantity selection controls.
- Add to Bag and Buy Now actions.
- Sticky purchase CTA.
- Shopping cart and checkout experience.
- Responsive interface design.
- Consistent typography and visual hierarchy.
